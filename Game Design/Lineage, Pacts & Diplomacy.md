# Lineage, Pacts & Diplomacy

People in the archipelago don't stand alone. They carry their families' names, debts, feuds and traditions, and they bind themselves to others with oaths that mean something.

## Family Lineage

- Every major character, and every Great House and pod, has a **family tree** in the game data: parents, siblings, children, spouses, adoptions, the dead.
- **What's inherited:** names, debts, feuds, land rights, faction loyalties, sometimes gem affinities. Children of Shardkin parents may grow crystal ([[Peoples, Clans & Cast#The Peoples]]).
- **Secrets in the tree:** Oriel's mother of Pod Saltmere; the other survivor from Marlo's wreck. Lineage is a lore source ([[Story Structure#Lore in the Story]]).
- **The captain's lineage** is left open at the start. The season can reveal it, and the core recognises *something* in your blood.
- **Kinship rule:** the system blocks romance between related characters, by blood or adoption within the same household. Lineage is tracked so this can never happen by accident.

## Found Family and Adoption

Formal adoption is a ritual in most cultures. The crew can become family: Nana already took in Tamsin. Adopting someone into the *Halyard*'s line makes them kin, and kinship blocks romance ([[Relationships & Life Events]]).

## Traditions and Culture

| Culture | Tradition | In play |
|---|---|---|
| **The Vey** | Every child is named after a storm they survived. The clan's dead are carved into the mast | Tamsin's real name is a storm. She hates it |
| **The Dorrits** | Each new keel is blessed with three drops of the builder's blood | The *Halyard* has Nana's blood in her keel, and her first husband's |
| **Tidefolk pods** | A child's first song is their name; pod-mothers decide marriages and wars | Oriel has no song-name. A pod can give her one |
| **Shardkin** | A gift of crystal shard means "I trust you with my life" | Giving it, or refusing it, is a defining moment |
| **Ironvale** | Debt is tattooed on the skin, and paid debts are inked over in black | Hesk's arms; Brannoc's lack of them |
| **The Luminari** | Purity oaths at coming of age; funerals by fire | Marlo still knows the oath words |
| **The Hollow Court** | Mutation is celebrated as a second birthday | The grey man's hand is a badge of honour |
| **Calm Shores** | The dead are given to the sea with a lamp | Marlo's ritual for anyone he loses |

**Writing rule:** a tradition only appears when someone is *living* it, at a wedding, a funeral, a coming of age, or a betrayal of it.

## Blood Pacts

Two people mix blood on a gem to bind an oath. The gem *remembers* the pact.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Making one** | A story choice with a crew member, an NPC or a faction leader, always with the terms spoken aloud |
| **What it does** | **Shared fate.** Your stress and theirs are linked ([[Psyche & Trauma#Psyche State (per character)]]), and you can feel when the other is in danger. Some pacts grant a shared Dispatch perk |
| **Keeping it** | Keeping a pact under pressure is one of the strongest loyalty events in the game |
| **Breaking it** | The gem cracks. Heavy strain on both, a psyche imprint, and everyone in that culture hears about it |
| **With the Wakened** | Constructs don't bleed. A pact with one uses core-oil instead, and nobody knows what that binds |

## Diplomatic Relationships

Factions, houses and pods deal in people as well as coin.

| Form | Example | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Marriage alliance** | A Luminari house offers its heir in marriage to the captain in exchange for protection | Political power and safety, but your current partner, your values and your crew all react ([[Relationships & Life Events#Marriage and Divorce]]) |
| **Fosterage** | A Tidefolk pod asks you to raise a child aboard as a bond of peace | A child aboard changes every risk you take |
| **Hostage exchange** | The Hollow Court and Luminari swap noble hostages through your ship | You hold the peace in your hold, and both sides watch |
| **Treaty and envoy** | You carry an envoy to negotiate a treaty | You can sabotage it, broker it, or rewrite it |
| **Sworn bond** | Becoming a pod-friend or House-sworn: a formal, public alliance | Doors open; enemies of that house become yours |

Political marriages are **always the player's choice** to accept, refuse or break, and the story treats a forced or loveless match with the weight it deserves.
