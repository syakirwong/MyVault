# Gear, Relics & Reliquaries

Gear in Stormbound is **about people, not numbers**. An item changes what a crew member can do on a call, how they feel, and which choices the story offers. The best items have names, histories and wills of their own.

## Tiers

| Tier | Where it comes from | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Common** | Ports, salvage, calls | +1 to a trait on certain calls (a fire axe for Force on fire calls) |
| **Fine** | Better ports, faction rewards | +1 to a trait on all calls, or a small perk |
| **Masterwork** | Forged by Nana or Brannoc ([[Workshop & Crafts]]) | Built *for one person*. Fits their profession and gives a perk, and they're attached to it |
| **Mythic** | Reliquaries (below), rare story rewards | Lattice-touched. Strong perks, but each **reacts to its bearer's gem**, with side effects |
| **Legendary** | One of each exists, found through story | Named relics with a history and a will. They unlock story choices and can change who their bearer becomes |

## Gear Slots (per crew member)

| Slot | Example | Mostly affects |
|---|---|---|
| **Tool** | Pilot's sextant, chemist's still, smith's hammer | Their profession: calls and crafting |
| **Gem-housing** | A forged collar or bracer around their gem | Gem strain ([[Crew & Bonds#Gem Strain]]) |
| **Garment** | A Luminari coat, Tidefolk sealskin, Ironvale leathers | Calls and how people react to them. Clothes are politics |
| **Keepsake** | A carved whistle, a letter, a lock of hair | The psyche: lowers stress, or triggers a wound ([[Psyche & Trauma]]) |

## Legendary Relics

Each has lore, a power, a price, and an opinion.

| Relic | History | Power | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Vey Storm-Sextant** | Tamsin's clan heirloom, lost with her family's ship | Lets the *Halyard* sail into the storm wall | Every use pulls the bearer toward the storm. Tamsin hears her family in it |
| **Saltmere Choir-Shell** | Sacred to Pod Saltmere; sings Lattice memories | Plays lore you can't get anywhere else, and opens Tidefolk doors | Oriel can't put it down. The songs are about her mother |
| **The Purifier's Knife** | A Luminari blade made to cut crystal out of Shardkin | Cuts through any gem, ward or Lattice seal | Every Shardkin who sees it knows what it's for. In Marlo's hands, it's a confession |
| **The Ironvale Debt-Chain** | The chain that bound Hesk's debt-gang | Whoever holds it can bind a person to a debt they can't escape | It can free Hesk, or own him |
| **The Halyard's Keel-Nail** | The first nail Nana drove into the ship, with her blood on it | The ship survives one wreck that should kill it | Pulling it out to use it is pulling out part of Nana |
| **The Sentinel's Heart** | The Lattice core-oil of the Tidewarden Sentinel | Wakes constructs, and maybe more | Taking it kills the Sentinel, or frees it. You won't know which until you do |

**Rules for legendaries:**
- **One bearer.** Giving a legendary to someone is a statement, and the rest of the crew react ([[Character Dynamics#4. Group Dynamics]]).
- **They choose back.** A relic can reject a bearer whose values don't match, and the bearer feels it.
- **They can be lost, stolen, broken or inherited.** A dead crew member's relic passes down by lineage or by your choice ([[Lineage, Pacts & Diplomacy#Family Lineage]]).
- **They're never upgraded with materials.** They *awaken* through story moments.

## Reliquaries (Mythic Chests)

Lattice-sealed chests from before the Shattering, found in wrecks, sunken cities, faction vaults and the deep places of the season.

**Opening one is a puzzle and a choice:**

| Way in | Needs | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Speak its name** | Lore: its rune-name, found in the Library or a song ([[Story Structure#Lore in the Story]]) | None, if you have the name |
| **Sing it open** | Tidefolk song (Oriel, or a pod ally) | The song may wake something else |
| **Blood-key** | A blood pact made with the reliquary ([[Lineage, Pacts & Diplomacy#Blood Pacts]]) | It's binding |
| **Dissolve the seal** | Hesk's acid ([[Workshop & Crafts]]) | May ruin part of what's inside |
| **Force it** | Brannoc and the forge | Lattice backlash: heavy gem strain for whoever's holding it |
| **The Purifier's Knife** | The legendary itself | Always works. Someone always remembers |

**Inside:**
- One **Mythic** item from the reliquary's themed table (a *Navigator's Reliquary* holds charts and sextants).
- Crafting materials.
- A **Lattice Memory**, which is lore.
- Sometimes, **not an item at all:** a sleeping Wakened, a voice, a curse.

**Fairness rules** (kept from the [[PKO Teardown]] avoid list):
- Reliquaries are **only found, never sold**. There are no paid chests of any kind ([[Tone & Content#Distribution Notes]]).
- Once you've studied a reliquary type in the Library, its **table and odds are shown**.
- **Pity rule:** the same Mythic item never drops twice in a row from one reliquary type.
- Forcing or dissolving a seal can damage the *contents*, never your own gear.

## Getting, Giving and Losing Gear

- **Sources:** story, Dispatch rewards, crafting, port traders, reliquaries, salvage, theft (Hesk), inheritance.
- **Gifts matter.** Giving someone gear changes their trust and affection toward you, and others notice who got what.
- **Gear tells stories.** Wearing a Luminari coat into Hollow Court waters is a choice, and people react to it.
