# Professions & Advancement

Every character has a **profession**, and grows through it across the season. Advancement isn't bought with points. It's **earned through work and decided by story**: a defining moment where the character, and often you, choose what they become.

## Professions

| Profession | Does | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot | Sails through danger; speed on sea calls | [[Dispatch Board]] |
| Navigator | Charts routes, finds hidden places | Calls, [[Voyage Calendar]] |
| Bosun | Keeps crew together and alive | Morale, stress ([[Psyche & Trauma]]) |
| Botanist | Grows medicine, food, toxins | [[Workshop & Crafts]] |
| Chemist | Mixes acids, antidotes, explosives | [[Workshop & Crafts]] |
| Smith | Forges tools, gem-housings, weapons | [[Workshop & Crafts]] |
| Shipwright | Repairs and upgrades the *Halyard* | Ship systems |
| Fighter | Holds doors, wins brawls | Dangerous calls |
| Medic | Treats injuries, slows illness | Injuries, Nana's illness |
| Diplomat | Talks factions down, makes deals | Faction standing |
| Scout / Thief | Infiltration, locks, quiet work | Theft and jail calls |
| Lore-keeper | Reads old script, remembers songs | Lore options (below) |

Recruits from ports bring professions the crew lacks (a Medic, a Diplomat). The crew can also learn new ones: Brannoc becoming a Smith is his whole arc.

## Advancement

**Tiers:** Novice → Journeyman → Master → **Path**.

- **Journeyman and Master** come from doing the work: calls of that profession, workshop time, and a Bond rank threshold ([[Crew & Bonds#Bonds (Dusk Scenes)]]).
- **Path** is a story event, a defining moment with two branches. What the character chooses depends on their values, their psyche, and what you've shown them.

## The Crew's Paths

| Crew | Profession | Path A | Path B | The defining moment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tamsin** | Pilot | **Stormchaser**: faster and reckless, can sail into storms no one else will | **Wayfinder**: steady, can lead other ships through | When she finally reaches the storm wall |
| **Marlo** | Bosun | **Warden**: can take a failed call's consequence onto himself | **Quartermaster**: extra supplies and an extra hour every Watch | When he's ordered to leave someone behind again |
| **Oriel** | Navigator + Botanist | **Songkeeper**: embraces her Tidefolk heritage, sings the old songs, unlocks lore | **Voidseer**: embraces the voices, and sees one call's outcome before you send anyone | When she learns who her mother was |
| **Hesk** | Chemist | **Physician**: antidotes and healing; a redemption he'd deny | **Rotwright**: mutagens for the Hollow Court, powerful and monstrous | When his old debt-gang comes calling |
| **Brannoc** | Fighter → Smith | **Master Smith**: finally the thinking job | **Pit Champion**: goes back to Ironvale, a legend again | When a crowd chants his name |
| **Nana** | Shipwright + Smith | **Master Shipwright**: the *Halyard*'s best upgrades | **Lattice-wright**: works core metal, which speeds up her illness | When she has to choose between the ship and time |

Paths change Dispatch traits, crafting options and story choices. Some lock out others: a Rotwright Hesk and a Songkeeper Oriel will not share a deck quietly.

## The Captain's Path

The captain's path follows the **core's alignment** ([[Choice & Consequence#State Model]]):

| Alignment | Captain becomes | Means |
|---|---|---|
| Radiant | **Commodore** | Leads Luminari-aligned fleets; order at a price |
| Corrupted | **Reaver** | Feared, free, feeding the change |
| Unbound | **Freeholder** | Owes no one; the Driftborn's friend |
