# Tone & Content

**Stormbound is a dark game with the full range of human feeling in it.** Violence, gore, death and nudity sit alongside calm, joy, purpose and peace. The darkness only lands because there is light to lose.

## The Emotional Range

| Register | Where it lives | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Violence and gore** | Failed calls, faction cruelty, gem mutation | A bargeman burning; a gem grown through a hand |
| **Chaos** | Storms, mutinies, the core waking | Calls arriving faster than you can answer them |
| **Calm** | Travel days, the Dusk rail, Nana's hull | Quiet scenes with no choices, just the sea |
| **Sadness** | Grief the crew carries; people you can't save | Marlo letting go of his captain's hand |
| **Joy** | Tamsin, small victories, shared meals | Laughing up the mast at a storm |
| **Purpose** | Marlo, the Luminari, finishing the voyage | "Come home. Every time." |
| **Nihilism** | Hesk, the Hollow Court, the indifferent sea | "Might as well be rich while nothing matters." |
| **Victory** | Calls won, enemies outplayed, a crew that survives | Episode finales |
| **Peace** | Endings that cost something | An ending where the world is quieter, but smaller |

Purpose and nihilism are **argued by the crew**, not by the narrator. Marlo and Hesk hold opposite views, and the player's choices decide which one the core and the ending side with ([[Choice & Consequence]]).

## Rules for Dark Content

1. **Violence has weight.** Every death is named or noticed by someone. No disposable bodies.
2. **Gore is for impact moments.** At most 1-2 graphic beats per scene. Constant gore numbs people, and a rare beat shocks them.
3. **Body horror comes from the lore.** Gem mutation is the game's signature dread ([[Crew & Bonds#Gem Strain]], [[Lore & Narrative]]).
4. **Nudity is non-sexual and contextual:** bathing, wounds being treated, Hollow Court mutation rites, bodies after a storm. It's shown with the same honesty as violence.
5. **Sexual content stays off-panel.** Intimacy is implied and cuts away. This keeps the game distributable (see below) and keeps the focus on character.
6. **Light needs room.** Every dark Dawn or Watch beat should have a counterweight within the same day, usually at Dusk.
7. **No cruelty as reward.** The game never celebrates cruelty to the helpless. Ruthless choices can win, but the story shows the cost.

## Rating and Content Controls

- **Target rating:** Mature (17+ / 18+). An age confirmation runs at first launch.
- **Content settings:**
  - **Full:** everything as written.
  - **Softened:** gore and nudity described less graphically. The same events happen, with the same consequences.
  - **Content notes:** an optional heads-up before scenes with self-harm, sexual violence (implied only, never shown), or harm to children (never shown).
- Content warnings appear on the title screen and the store page.

## Distribution Notes

*Verify against current store policies before launch. These are general expectations, not checked rules.*

| Channel | Expectation |
|---|---|
| Web / PWA | The most flexible. An age gate plus content settings is enough |
| Steam | Mature violence and nudity are allowed with content descriptors |
| iOS App Store / Google Play | Violence and non-sexual nudity are usually acceptable at a 17+ / Mature rating. Explicit sexual content is not |
| Ads and some payment processors | May restrict mature content, which suits a premium (pay once) model ([[Stormbound GDD#Open Questions]]) |
