# Choice & Consequence

## State Model

| State | Range | Changed by | Read by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Flags** | true / false | Specific choices ("towed the Driftborn boat") | Later scenes and calls |
| **Crew loyalty** | 0-10 per crew member, starts 5 | Choices they care about, dispatch outcomes, Dusk scenes | Their lines, whether they stay, bond perks |
| **Core alignment** | Radiant / Corrupted / Unbound points | Choices about the core and how you treat people | Endings, the core's voice, faction reactions |
| **Core stability** | 0-100 | Using the core's power, risky choices | Endings; at low stability it acts on its own |
| **Faction standing** | -3 to +3 per major faction | Deals, betrayals, contracts | Who helps you, who hunts you |
| **Captain's reputation** | Tags such as *Merciful*, *Ruthless*, *Reckless* | Patterns across several choices (from [[Behavioral Identity]]) | Crew dialogue: they comment on who you're becoming |
| **Relationships** | Friend / rival / romance stage / married / separated / ex, plus relationship strain | Dusk answers, loyalty to partners, betrayals | Partner scenes, jealousy, weddings, divorces ([[Relationships & Life Events]]) |
| **Legal status** | Free / wanted in a port / jailed (per crew member and captain) | Failed illegal calls, Luminari standing | Who's available, jail arcs, which ports are safe |

Crew loyalty at **0** means the crew member leaves at the next port. At **10** they'll follow you into something they disagree with, once.

## Consequence Types

| Type | Example | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Immediate** | Lie to the Luminari, and the inspector's eyes narrow | Shown in the same scene |
| **Delayed** | Skip the tow on day 2, and on day 5 you find the boat wrecked | Lands 1-3 episodes later, and the game reminds you of the original choice when it does |
| **Cumulative** | Overrule the crew three times, and you earn the *Tyrant* reputation | Built from patterns, so no single choice is punished |
| **Irreversible** | A crew member leaves or dies | Telegraphed beforehand. Never a surprise from a choice that looked minor |

## Feedback Rules

- When a choice moves loyalty, show a quiet line on the panel: **"Tamsin saw that."** No numbers mid-scene.
- At the end of each day, the **Captain's Log** lists what changed, in plain words ("Marlo trusts you more. The Luminari are watching you.").
- At the end of each episode, a **Consequence Ledger** shows the major choices and what they led to, including the ones still waiting to land ("This may matter later").
- Never hide that a choice mattered. Hide *how much* until it lands.

## Writing Rules

1. **No fake choices in key moments.** At most 30% of options in a scene may be flavour-only, and never at a marked decision point.
2. **Values, not right answers.** Every option at a decision point should be defensible by at least one crew member.
3. **Every episode needs** at least 3 choices that pay off within the same episode and at least 2 that pay off later.
4. **Don't punish curiosity.** Asking questions or exploring dialogue never costs loyalty.
5. **Timed choices** only at moments of real pressure, at most 2 per episode. They can be turned off in settings ([[Motion Comic Presentation#Accessibility]]).
