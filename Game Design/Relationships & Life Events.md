# Relationships & Life Events

The captain lives a whole life across the season: friends, rivals, lovers, a marriage, perhaps a divorce, and perhaps a cell. All of it runs through the same state as everything else ([[Choice & Consequence]]).

## Relationship Types

| Type | How it starts | Can become |
|---|---|---|
| **Friendship** | Answering a crew member "as a friend" at Dusk; standing by them in a dispute | Best friend (rank 5 Bond), or a betrayal that hurts more |
| **Rivalry** | Overruling someone repeatedly; siding against them | Grudging respect, or enmity |
| **Romance** | Answering "as something more" at Dusk (the *spark*) | Courting → Together → Married, or heartbreak |
| **Marriage** | Proposing once Together, at a port (episode 3 or later) | A lasting partnership, or separation and divorce |
| **Enmity** | Betrayal, abandonment, a death they blame you for | Revenge, mutiny, or a reckoning scene |
| **Crew with crew** | They have their own lives: friendships, feuds and romances between crew members happen with or without you | Their marriage, their breakup, their duel |

Friendship and romance are **separate tracks**. You can be someone's closest friend without romance, and a romance can fail while the friendship survives, or not.

## Romance

**Stages:** Spark → Courting → Together → Committed or Married.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who | Any adult crew member, plus some recurring NPCs (e.g. the Driftborn thief's older sister, a Luminari inspector). Available regardless of the captain's gender |
| Their own minds | Characters have their own wants and limits. **Oriel** isn't interested in romance; her route is a deep friendship. **Hesk** will flirt with anyone and commit to no one, unless you change him |
| Jealousy | Courting two people at once is possible. Both notice eventually, and there's a scene |
| Partner effects | The partner gets extra Dusk scenes and a loyalty floor (it won't drop below 3). But enemies now know where to hurt you |
| Intimacy | Implied and off-panel, per [[Tone & Content#Rules for Dark Content]] |

## Marriage and Divorce

- **Marriage:** a proposal choice once Together, then a wedding at a port with the crew present, faction guests, and somebody objecting. Marriage changes endings: your spouse's survival and values weigh on the core's final state.
- **Strain:** choices against your partner's values, neglecting them for several days, or betraying them adds **relationship strain**. It works like gem strain, with the same bands: steady → shifting → unstable → critical.
- **Separation:** at critical strain, a confrontation scene. You can repair it, separate, or divorce.
- **Divorce has consequences:** the ex may leave the ship, or stay aboard as an ex. That creates tense Dusk scenes and changed crew dynamics, and other crew take sides.
- **Death of a partner** is possible only through explicit high-stakes choices ([[Crew & Bonds#Losing Crew]]). It's the heaviest grief scene in the game.

## Jail

| Trigger | What happens |
|---|---|
| Crew caught on an illegal call (e.g. the smuggling job fails) | They're held in the Tidewater cells and unavailable until freed |
| The captain caught, or Luminari standing reaches −3 | **Jail arc:** days pass while the first mate runs the Dispatch Board in their own style, and you play scenes from the cell |

**Ways out:** pay bail (debt), break out (a high-risk call that leaves you wanted in that port), stand trial (a courtroom scene where your reputation and witnesses matter), or make a deal (owe a faction, inform on someone). Every option changes who trusts you.

## Other Life Events

| Event | Example |
|---|---|
| Injury and scars | A failed fire call leaves a permanent burn. The character is changed, and people react to it |
| Debt collection | Mother Brisk's people come for payment, sometimes violently |
| Exile | Banned from a port after a breakout or a betrayal |
| Funerals | Burial at sea for a lost crew member; the crew remembers them in later scenes |
| Family | A crew member's estranged child turns up at a port |
| Addiction and recovery | Using the core's power to fix things starts to feel very good |
