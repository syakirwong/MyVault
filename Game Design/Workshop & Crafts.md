# Workshop & Crafts

The *Halyard* carries three workstations: a **sea-garden**, a **chemistry bench** and a **forge**. During the Watch, any crew member you don't send on a call can work one instead ([[Dispatch Board]]). Crafting competes with jobs for your people's time, and that trade-off is the strategy.

## The Three Disciplines

| Discipline | Station | Lead crew | Makes | Key trait |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Botany** | Sea-garden: kelp tanks and potted island plants on the aft deck | **Oriel** | Medicines, sedatives, food, toxins, dyes | Attunement |
| **Chemistry** | Bench below decks, ventilated badly | **Hesk** | Acids, explosives, antidotes, stimulants, gem-solvent | Acuity |
| **Smithing** | Forge in the bow, with Nana's tools | **Nana Dorrit**, with **Brannoc** as apprentice | Gem-housings, tools, lockpicks, weapons, ship repairs | Force + Precision |

Why these leads:
- **Oriel** grows Tidefolk kelp far better than any land-raised person should. It's a hint at her heritage ([[Peoples, Clans & Cast#The Crew, Reworked]]).
- **Hesk** learned chemistry from salvage: rot, rust and acid. His Entropy gem makes reactions run fast and unpredictably.
- **Brannoc** apprenticing at the forge is the "thinking job" he's always wanted, and his purpose arc ([[Psyche & Trauma#The Crew's Wounds]]).

Specialists can also be recruited at ports, such as a Shardkin crystal-chemist from the Glasshouse or a Tidefolk kelp-singer from Pod Saltmere.

## How Crafting Works

- **Botany is slow.** Plants grow over days and need tending. Neglect them and they die, while a rare bloom is worth waiting for. Some plants **only grow near the core**, and they grow strange.
- **Chemistry is fast and risky.** Combining reagents takes hours. A failed mix can burn, poison or explode. This is the one place a crafting accident can injure someone, and it's always telegraphed.
- **Smithing is steady.** It needs materials and time, and quality depends on who's at the anvil.

**Materials** come from Dispatch rewards, salvage, ports and the garden itself.

## What Crafted Things Do

| Use | Examples |
|---|---|
| **On calls** | Fire-retardant paste (+1 Resilience on fire calls), lockpicks (open a new option on theft calls), smoke bombs |
| **In story choices** | Poison, a forged seal, a sedative slipped into a drink. Unlock options that otherwise don't exist, with moral weight |
| **For gem strain** | Forged **gem-housings** reduce strain from jobs. **Gem-solvent** can cut a Shardkin's crystal out, which is what the Luminari's "purification" uses. Using it is a heavy choice |
| **For the psyche** | Sedatives and tinctures lower stress quickly, but dependence is possible ([[Psyche & Trauma]], [[Relationships & Life Events#Other Life Events]]) |
| **For jail** | Lockpicks, acid for the bars, a sleeping draught for the guard ([[Relationships & Life Events#Jail]]) |
| **For the ship** | Hull patches, rigging and the bilge seam ([[Voyage Calendar#Voyage Pressure]]) |

## Rules

1. **Every recipe has a story use**, not just a stat. If it only changes a number, cut it.
2. **Discovery over recipe lists.** Recipes are found through people, books, salvage and experiments, not menus.
3. **Consent matters.** Drugging or "fixing" a crew member without their agreement is possible, and the story treats it as the violation it is.
4. **The darker the craft, the higher the cost.** Poisons and solvents are effective, and characters remember who made and used them.
