# Story Structure

## Format

| Unit | Length | Contains |
|---|---|---|
| **Season** | 5 episodes | One full arc about the Lattice core |
| **Episode** | One voyage, about 7 in-game days, 60-90 min | 7 Dawn scenes, 7 Dispatch watches, 7 Dusk bonds, a finale |
| **Scene** | 3-8 minutes | 2-5 choices, at least one that matters |

## Branching Model: Branch and Bottleneck, with Persistent State

Fully branching stories double in size with every fork, so Stormbound uses:

- **Bottlenecks:** each episode reaches the same major event (the finale), but *who is there, what they think of you, and what state the core is in* differ.
- **State-driven variation:** most consequences are carried by state (flags, loyalty, alignment, faction standing; see [[Choice & Consequence]]). Scenes read that state and change lines, who shows up, and which options exist.
- **Real forks at episode ends:** each finale has 3 outcomes that carry forward and change the next episode's opening and available crew.
- **Irreversible losses** (a crew member leaving or dying) are real branches. Later episodes are written with that character's absence in mind.

**Budget rule:** about 70% of each episode's text is shared, about 30% is state-variant. Plan writing time on that basis.

## Season 1 Arc

| Episode | Voyage | Core question |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Salvage Rights | Port Haven → the Stormspire wreck | What did you pull out of the water, and who will you tell? |
| 2. The Lantern Coast | Luminari waters | Will you let them "restore" it, and what does restoring cost? |
| 3. Hollow Tide | Hollow Court territory | What happens when you feed it? |
| 4. Driftwater | The Driftborn flotillas | Does the core get a voice of its own? |
| 5. Convergence | The heart of the old Lattice | What does it become, and who's still beside you? |

The core's state at the finale (Radiant, Corrupted or Unbound, and how *stable* it is) plus who remains aboard selects the ending. The season has 9+ endings, made from 3 alignments × 3 stability bands, each flavoured by crew.

## Episode 1 — Salvage Rights

| Day | Dawn (story) | Key choice | Consequence carried |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | You win the *Halyard* in a debt deal. Meet the crew | Accept the loan shark's terms, or take a Hollow Court advance | Debt holder (a story antagonist) |
| 2 | Leave Port Haven. A Driftborn boat begs a tow | Tow them (lose half a day) or sail on | Driftborn standing; a call that returns on day 5 |
| 3 | Arrive at the Stormspire wreck. The Tidewarden Sentinel guards it | Fight past it, sneak in, or negotiate with its keeper-runes | Who gets hurt; how intact the Sentinel is (it returns in ep 2) |
| 4 | Inside the wreck you find the core. It *speaks* to one crew member | Who carries it? | That crew member's gem starts mutating |
| 5 | A Luminari cutter hails you, asking for "inspection" | Hide it, hand it over, or lie | Luminari standing; trust from the crew member who carries it |
| 6 | Mutiny brews. Two crew want to sell the core | Side with them, overrule them, or put it to a vote | Loyalty; someone may leave at Port Haven |
| 7 | Finale: back at Port Haven, all three factions are waiting | Who do you bring it to, or do you keep it? | **3 episode endings** → episode 2 opening |

Between Dawn scenes, the Dispatch Board ([[Dispatch Board]]) keeps the world moving with salvage jobs, distress calls and faction contracts. Some calls are tied to the story (day 2's tow comes back on day 5).

## Writing Guidelines

- A scene opens on action or a question, never on exposition.
- Every crew member speaks in at least one scene per day, even if only in a line.
- Show lore through objects and people (a cracked gem, a faction tattoo), not lectures. Pull from [[Lore & Narrative]].
- Choice text states the *intent*, not the exact words ("Lie about the core", not a full sentence), so players never get a line they didn't mean.
