# Crew & Bonds

## The Captain (You)

You choose a name and pronouns. You have no gem of your own. Instead you are the one the **core** listens to, so your choices drive its alignment. Your stats are your crew.

## The Crew of the Halyard

Traits use the Six Stats from [[Stats System]] on a 1-5 scale. Each crew member's people, clan and history are in [[Peoples, Clans & Cast]]; their crafts (Oriel botany, Hesk chemistry, Nana and Brannoc smithing) are in [[Workshop & Crafts]].

| Crew | Gem | Role | Strong traits | Flaw | Wants |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bosun Marlo** | Aegis | First mate, keeps everyone alive | Resilience 5, Acuity 3 | Won't leave anyone behind, even when he should | A crew that comes home, every time |
| **Tamsin Vey** | Tempest | Pilot, fast and brilliant | Precision 5, Instinct 4 | Can't wait; acts before orders | To outrun the storm that took her family's ship |
| **Oriel** | Void | Navigator who sees things others can't | Acuity 5, Attunement 4 | Keeps secrets, even helpful ones | To learn what the Void keeps whispering to her |
| **Hesk** | Entropy | Ex-salvager, cynical, very good | Instinct 4, Precision 3, Acuity 3 | Greed. Always has an angle | To never be poor again |
| **Brannoc** | Kinetics | Deckhand and muscle | Force 5, Resilience 4 | Temper | To be trusted with more than lifting |
| **Nana Dorrit** | Synthesis | Old shipwright, held the ship together for forty years | Attunement 5, Acuity 4 | Stubborn and frail | To see the *Halyard* outlive her |

**Relationships between crew** matter on the Dispatch Board:

| Pair | Relation | Effect when sent together |
|---|---|---|
| Marlo + Tamsin | Mentor and protégé | +10% |
| Hesk + Marlo | Mutual distrust | −10%, and a chance of an argument scene |
| Oriel + Nana Dorrit | Quiet friendship | +10% on Attunement calls |
| Brannoc + Tamsin | Rivalry | +5% speed, −5% success |

Choices can change these relationships. A rivalry can become respect.

## Bonds (Dusk Scenes)

Each evening you spend time with **one** crew member. Who you pick, and how you answer them, raises their **Bond** (rank 1-5).

| Rank | Unlocks |
|---|---|
| 2 | Their backstory scene |
| 3 | A Dispatch perk (e.g. Marlo: once per episode, a failed call becomes a partial success) |
| 4 | They'll argue for you in a crew dispute |
| 5 | Their personal arc's climax, where the outcome depends on your choices |

You can't max everyone in one season. That's deliberate: who you get close to is itself a choice.

## Gem Strain

Repurposes gem stability from the [[Combat System#Gem Stability and Mutation|old combat design]].

- Every dispatch adds **strain** to that crew member's gem (5-20 by risk). Resting a day removes 15.
- Strain bands follow the old mutation thresholds:

| Strain | State | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0-24 | Steady | Normal |
| 25-49 | Shifting | A small trait change and a short scene ("Tamsin's hands won't stop sparking") |
| 50-74 | Unstable | A mutation event: a choice that can permanently change a trait, for better or worse |
| 75-99 | Critical | Big story event. The gem may give them a new ability or start to consume them |
| 100 | Broken | They lose the gem. Major story fork: they may leave, or become someone new |

This is the strategic tension: your best people are the ones you want to send, and the ones you can't keep sending.

## Losing Crew

- **Leaving:** loyalty hits 0. They go at the next port, with a farewell scene.
- **Captured:** a failed high-risk call. A rescue becomes possible in a later episode.
- **Death:** only from an explicit, telegraphed high-stakes choice ("Send Brannoc to hold the door"). Never from random chance.
