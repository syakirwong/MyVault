# Voyage Calendar

## Day Structure

| Part | Length | Player does |
|---|---|---|
| **Dawn** | 1 story scene | Make choices ([[Story Structure]]) |
| **Watch** | 8 in-game hours | Run the [[Dispatch Board]] |
| **Dusk** | 1 bond scene | Spend time with one crew member ([[Crew & Bonds#Bonds (Dusk Scenes)]]) |
| **Night** | Summary | Read the Captain's Log: consequences, strain changes, loyalty shifts ([[Choice & Consequence#Feedback Rules]]) |

Saves happen automatically at each part of the day.

## Voyage Pressure

- **Deadline:** each episode has a hard story deadline (Episode 1: back at Port Haven by day 7). Days are the scarcest resource.
- **Supplies:** food and repair stock go down each day and come back from calls. Running low forces hard choices: cut rations (−loyalty), sell something, or take a risky contract.
- **Travel days:** some days the ship is at sea, so only sea-based calls appear. Those days are more personal: more Dusk options, fewer jobs.

## Why This Works

The calendar turns every other system into a trade-off: *one more job, or one more evening with Oriel before she makes her decision?* That tension is the strategy, without combat.
