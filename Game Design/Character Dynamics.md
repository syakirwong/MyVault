# Character Dynamics

Relationships in Stormbound are **one-sided, layered, secret-laden and alive**. They change when you're not looking, and the crew's feelings about each other matter as much as their feelings about you.

## 1. One-Sided, Layered Relationships

Every pair is two relationships, A's view of B and B's view of A, each measured on several axes:

| Axis | Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Trust** | Will I rely on you? | Hesk respects Marlo but doesn't trust him |
| **Affection** | Do I care about you? | Marlo cares about Hesk out of guilt Hesk can't see |
| **Respect** | Do I rate you? | Brannoc respects Tamsin and would never say so |
| **Fear** | Am I wary of you? | Everyone, a little, of Brannoc |
| **Obligation** | Do I owe you, or you me? | Tamsin owes Nana her life, and resents it |
| **Attraction** | Am I drawn to you? | Tamsin to Oriel, before either knows their families' history |

Axes move separately. High affection with low trust is the most dramatic combination in the game: *I love you and I can't rely on you.*

## 2. How the Crew See Each Other (Episode 1 start)

Read across: how the **row** sees the **column**.

| | Marlo | Tamsin | Oriel | Hesk | Brannoc | Nana |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Marlo** | — | His second chance. Watches her too closely | Strange, but steady in a storm | Guilt. Kind to him and can't say why | A good man who thinks he isn't | The one person who knew him young |
| **Tamsin** | Smothering. She'd die for him | — | Can't stop looking at her | Fun to drink with. Wouldn't trust him with a rope | Rival, and annoyingly good | Gran. Owes her everything, resents it |
| **Oriel** | Safe | Too bright, like looking at the sun | — | He hears the cracks in people too | Gentle, and nobody notices | Knows something about me. I can feel it |
| **Hesk** | Smells like a Luminari patrol | An easy mark, and he likes her anyway | The only one who doesn't stare at his spine | — | Ironvale. Never turn your back on Ironvale | Pays fair. He respects that |
| **Brannoc** | Wants his respect more than anything | Rival; secretly impressed | Wants to protect her. She doesn't need it | Knows who owns his debt. Hasn't said | — | Finally, a teacher who lets him think |
| **Nana** | Old friend. Almost more, once. Still almost | Her girl. Terrified for her | Her promise to a dead woman | A thief, but an honest one | Her apprentice. Proud of him | — |
## 3. Secrets: Who Knows What

The game tracks a **knowledge graph**: every secret, and who holds it.

| Secret | Belongs to | Who knows at the start | If it spreads |
|---|---|---|---|
| Served on purification patrols | Marlo | Nobody. Nana suspects | Hesk's view of Marlo collapses. Violence or forgiveness |
| Mother was of Pod Saltmere | Oriel | Nana | Tamsin learns her clan hunted Oriel's people |
| Ill, and getting worse | Nana | Oriel, from the medicines she's been asked to brew | Tamsin learns Oriel kept it from her |
| Knows who holds Hesk's debt | Brannoc | Nobody | Hesk wonders why Brannoc never told him |
| Chasing the storm wall on purpose | Tamsin | Brannoc, who saw her charts | Marlo turns over-protective in a way that breaks them |
| Still pays the gang that owned him | Hesk | Nobody | Nobody understands it, least of all Hesk |

**How secrets move:**
- **Gossip:** close pairs share what they know. Each Night, secrets can spread along high-trust links.
- **Pressure:** at high stress, characters blurt things out ([[Psyche & Trauma#Breaking Points]]).
- **You:** learn a secret, then keep it, reveal it, use it as leverage, or trade it to a faction.
- Every secret has a **worst moment**: the scene where it would do the most damage if it came out. The story aims for it.

## 4. Group Dynamics

- **Cliques form.** The *old guard* (Marlo and Nana) and the *young bloods* (Tamsin and Brannoc), with Hesk and Oriel drifting between them. Cliques change with events.
- **Votes and disputes.** When the crew decides something together, like Episode 1's day-6 vote on selling the core, each character votes from their own relationships and values, not their loyalty to you alone.
- **Triangles.** Romantic (two crew drawn to the same person, or to you), loyalty (a character torn between you and their friend), and blood (Tamsin, Oriel, and a clan history neither chose).
- **Caught in the middle.** At key moments a character has to pick a side between you and someone they love. What they pick is computed from the axes, then written as a scene.

## 5. Dynamics Change Without You

- **Each Night**, crew-to-crew relationships drift based on the day: who went on calls together, how those went, who you favoured, what was said.
- **Below Decks scenes:** short comic scenes between crew, with no captain present. The player overhears the crew's lives, with their own fights, jokes and romances.
- **They react to your relationships:** favouritism is noticed, a crew romance with you stirs jealousy, and an ex stays aboard and sides against you ([[Relationships & Life Events]]).

## 6. Power and Hierarchy

- **Chain of command:** the first mate can be challenged, replaced or overthrown. Who's second matters most when you're jailed ([[Relationships & Life Events#Jail]]).
- **Debts and favours** between crew are tracked like money: Hesk lends Brannoc something; Brannoc owes, and resents owing.
- **Mutiny** isn't a meter. It's a coalition: it happens when enough people with enough trust in each other stop trusting you.

## Design Rules

1. **Every pair has at least one bond and one tension.**
2. **No static relationships.** Every pair changes at least once per episode.
3. **Legible in hindsight.** Below Decks scenes and the [[Choice & Consequence#Feedback Rules|Consequence Ledger]] show *why* a dynamic shifted. Raw numbers are never shown.
4. **Scope control:** 6 crew means 30 one-way relationships. Writers hand-craft **8 story pairs**. The rest use reaction templates driven by the axes.
