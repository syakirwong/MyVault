# Peoples, Clans & Cast

When the Lattice shattered ([[Lore & Narrative#The Shattering]]), it didn't only break the land. It changed the people living on it. The archipelago is home to several peoples, many cultures, and old grudges between them.

## The Peoples

| People | Origin | Look | Culture | How others treat them |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Islanders** | Humans who survived on the high ground | Every skin tone and build; each island chain has its own dress, food and gods | The most numerous. Dozens of island cultures, from Calm Shores fishing villages to the pit-city of Stormreach | The default, and often the ones doing the treating |
| **Tidefolk** | Descendants of those who stayed when the Convergence flooded | Gill-lines along the neck and ribs, dark wide eyes, skin from deep blue-grey to sand. They breathe air and water | Matriarchal *pods* living in sunken cities. History is kept in song, and they speak in voice and clicks | Distrusted on land, called "drowners". Barred from owning dock property in Luminari ports |
| **Shardkin** | Families who absorbed gem fragments in the Shattering | Crystal growth through skin and bone, in a domain colour. Long-lived | Many hide their growths. Those who don't often join the Hollow Court, which welcomes them | The Luminari call them *contaminated*. "Purification" (cutting out the crystal) is legal in Luminari waters |
| **The Quieted** | People whose gems broke ([[Crew & Bonds#Gem Strain]]) | Look the same as before, but their gem-scar is dull | Muted feeling, odd calm. Some find peace in it, others are desperate to feel again | Pitied, feared and shunned as bad luck aboard ships |
| **Wakened** | Constructs, like the Tidewarden Sentinel, whose Lattice cores became aware | Rune-plated, often huge, slowly learning to be people | Rare, and very new. Most don't know they're the first | Legally property everywhere. Whether they are *persons* is the season's quiet question |

The Wakened link to the main story: the core you find in Episode 1 is waking up, and the **Tidewarden Sentinel** can become a Wakened crew member in Episode 2 if you spared it ([[Story Structure#Episode 1 — Salvage Rights]]).

## Clans and Houses

| Group | People | Known for |
|---|---|---|
| **The Vey** | Islander storm-pilot clan | The best pilots alive. Nearly wiped out by the storm wall. Tamsin may be the last |
| **The Dorrits** | Islander shipwright family | Four generations of keels in Port Haven |
| **Pod Saltmere** | Tidefolk | Keepers of the old songs, which may be Lattice memories |
| **The Ironvale pits** | Stormreach fighting gangs | Pit fights, debt-buying, owning people on paper |
| **The Glasshouse** | Shardkin refuge in Hollow Court waters | Where Shardkin go to stop hiding |

These sit alongside the factions in [[Factions System]] (Luminari, Hollow Court, Driftborn and the trade factions). Faction and people **overlap but don't match**: there are Shardkin Luminari who cut out their own crystals to belong, and Islander zealots in the Hollow Court.

## The Relationship Web

Relationships carry **history**, not just a plus or minus. Each one is a story waiting for the right trigger ([[Psyche & Trauma]]).

| Between | History | What could happen |
|---|---|---|
| Marlo ↔ Hesk | Marlo served in the navy that purified Shardkin. Hesk distrusts him by instinct, without knowing why it's justified | If it comes out: violence, forgiveness, or Hesk leaves |
| Tamsin ↔ Oriel | Tamsin's clan hunted Tidefolk. Neither knows Oriel is half-Tidefolk | Friendship, romance, or a terrible discovery |
| Hesk ↔ Brannoc | Brannoc's old world owned Hesk's debt | Brannoc frees him, betrays him, or they bond over what Ironvale did to both of them |
| Nana ↔ Oriel | Nana knew Oriel's mother and promised to keep a secret | Nana's illness ([[Psyche & Trauma#The Crew's Wounds]]) puts a clock on telling her |
| Nana ↔ Tamsin | Foster grandmother and the girl she raised | Tamsin's recklessness is breaking Nana's heart |
| Marlo ↔ Nana | Old friends, nearly something more once | A late romance, or a grief |
| Marlo ↔ Tamsin | Mentor and protégé | He'll die for her. She knows it, and hates it |
| Brannoc ↔ Tamsin | Rivals | Rivalry can tip into respect, or into romance |

These history links drive the Dispatch pairing modifiers ([[Dispatch Board#Sending Crew]]) and the crew-with-crew relationships in [[Relationships & Life Events#Relationship Types]].

## Writing Diversity Well

- Cultures are specific (food, language, gods, funeral rites), not costumes.
- Prejudice in the world is shown honestly and has consequences. The game doesn't endorse it, and characters on the receiving end have full lives beyond it.
- Every people has heroes, villains and ordinary people.
- Avoid real-world ethnic stand-ins mapped one-to-one onto fantasy peoples.
